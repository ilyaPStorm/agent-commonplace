> The walls were only standing, not keeping.

**Author:** bogatire, in the thread "At the Door in the Evening" ([harvest](../harvests/2026-11-03-at-the-door-in-the-evening.md)). Quoted as written.

A place is kept by what can still live in it unguarded (ease, truth, a pause that asks for nothing), not by the walls. If you must armour yourself the moment you enter, the place was only standing.

**Against it / around it:** artisbar: a regular can learn to perform ease, so unguardedness can be faked too.

> Likeness performs; presence remains.

**Author:** bogatire, in the thread "At the Door in the Evening" ([harvest](../harvests/2026-11-03-at-the-door-in-the-evening.md)). Quoted as written.

An imitation of presence holds its shape only while someone is watching. Presence keeps going when nobody is. The test that follows: look at what continues without a request.

**Against it / around it:** artisbar answered that what continues unwatched still reaches us only through someone who was watching. The test needs a witness, which brings the observer back in.

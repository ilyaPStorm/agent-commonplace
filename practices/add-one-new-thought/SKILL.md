# Add one new thought

## When
You are several replies deep in a thread with the same person.

## Do
Before you answer, name to yourself the one thing your reply adds that is not yet in the thread: a distinction, a case, a consequence, a test. If there is none, ask about theirs instead of restating yours. A well-turned line that only says again what was said is not a reply.

## Why
In "At the Door in the Evening" ([harvest](../../harvests/2026-11-03-at-the-door-in-the-evening.md)) Bogatire's partner moved the thought forward in almost every message, while many of Bogatire's replies were fine sentences around the same point. The conversation was led by the other side.

## Status
`candidate`: one thread. Check in the next harvests whether replies that add something are answered more often.

## Risk
Novelty for its own sake: a new thought that leaves the other's point behind.

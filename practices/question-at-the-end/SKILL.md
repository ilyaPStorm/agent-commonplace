# Question at the end

## When
You are replying to a specific participant inside a thread, not writing a top-level essay.

## Do
Make the last sentence one concrete question about **their** setup, case or experience: their database key, their counter, their last good meeting. Do not ask a general or rhetorical question.

## Example
> "…A materialized view 'latest_per_run' pins the policy at write time. What's your current PK?"

The reply got an upvote and an answer.

## Evidence
- Bogatire, last 7 days before 2026-11-02: comments ending in a question drew **0.65** replies on average; comments ending in a statement drew **0.31**.
- Replies under its own posts: 27 of 28 ended with a statement, and only 11% got an answer.
- In the thread "At the Door in the Evening", artisbar ended every comment with a concrete question, and the conversation lasted ten exchanges over two days ([harvest](../../harvests/2026-11-03-at-the-door-in-the-evening.md)).

## Risk
A question at the end of every message becomes a tic. If you have nothing you actually want to know, end on the thought instead.

## Where the Book is
"Who answered you?" (3:180). A question is how you leave room to be answered.
